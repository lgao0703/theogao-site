Hi, I'm Theo (Long Gao).

I work where medical devices meet software and security. I'm a project engineer at TÜV Rheinland in Shenzhen, where I assess the cybersecurity and compliance of connected medical devices and software as / in a medical device (SaMD / SiMD) — infusion pumps, CT and X-ray systems, endoscopes, patient monitors.

My background runs from biology to biomedical engineering: a BSc in Biology at Georgia State University (with a double degree in Bioengineering from Southwest Jiaotong University), then an MSc in Biomedical Engineering at the National University of Singapore, concentrating in medical robotics.
